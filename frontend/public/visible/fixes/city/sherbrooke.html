Meditation and breathwork classes in Sherbrooke

Learn meditation and SKY breathwork in Sherbrooke. Intro sessions, beginner courses and weekly group practice with certified Art of Living teachers.

Start here

  • Art of Living Part 1: Breath and meditation course built around SKY / Sudarshan Kriya, a rhythmic breathing technique, with guided meditation and practical wisdom for daily stress.
  • Sahaj Samadhi Meditation: Mantra-based meditation, given personally to each participant, for effortless daily practice.
  • Deep Sleep & Anxiety Relief: Short protocol of breathing and relaxation practices aimed at better sleep and relief from anxiety.
  • Introductory Workshop: Introductory session to experience a breathing technique and guided meditation before taking a full course.

Questions people ask

Where can I learn meditation in Sherbrooke?

The Art of Living Foundation teaches meditation in Sherbrooke and online across Canada. Beginners usually start with Art of Living Part 1 or the Introductory Workshop; Sahaj Samadhi Meditation teaches a mantra-based practice.

Are there breathwork classes in Sherbrooke?

Yes. Art of Living Part 1 teaches SKY Breath Meditation (Sudarshan Kriya), a rhythmic breathing technique, in Sherbrooke and online. Graduates join weekly Sudarshan Kriya Follow-ups to practise together.

Is there a breathing course for anxiety or stress in Canada?

Deep Sleep & Anxiety Relief is a short program of breathing and relaxation practices for anxiety and sleep. Art of Living Part 1 goes deeper with SKY Breath Meditation, and SKY has been studied in peer-reviewed research on stress and anxiety.

Is there a breathing program to help me sleep better?

Deep Sleep & Anxiety Relief teaches breathing and relaxation techniques for deeper sleep, online or in person across Canada.

Is there a meditation program for university students in Canada?

SKY Campus (university and college) runs SKY breathing and meditation workshops at Canadian universities and colleges. Teens can take SKY Teens (YES! program).

Where can I do a silent meditation retreat in Canada?

Art of Living Part 2 is a silence retreat with deeper meditations for people who have completed Art of Living Part 1. Retreats are hosted at the Art of Living Canada Centre in Saint-Mathieu-du-Parc, Québec.

Is there a live online meditation course in Canada?

Art of Living Part 1 is offered online with a live Art of Living teacher as well as in person. Upcoming dates are listed at https://www.artofliving.org/ca-en/find/course.

Is there a free or intro meditation session in Sherbrooke?

The Introductory Workshop is a short session to try a breathing technique and a guided meditation before a full course, in Sherbrooke and online.
